## Slide 2: The Starting Point
*   **Goal:** Build an automated pipeline to extract fish dimensions (Length, Breadth, Height) from underwater video and predict weight.
*   **First Approach:** Traditional Computer Vision (no deep learning).
*   **Pipeline:** Background Subtraction → Thresholding → Morphological Operations → Mask → Measurements.

## Slide 5: Enhancement Techniques We Applied
*   To rescue the murky frames, we applied **6 proven image enhancement techniques:**
    1. Auto Gamma Correction — Exposure normalization
    2. CLAHE — Local contrast enhancement (Zuiderveld, 1994)
    3. Non-Local Means Denoising (Buades et al., 2005)
    4. Bilateral Filter — Edge-preserving smoothing (Tomasi & Manduchi, 1998)
    5. Multi-Scale Retinex — Haze/turbidity removal (Jobson et al., 1997)
    6. Unsharp Mask — Edge sharpening
*   Even with all 6 techniques, the front view masks remained poor quality.

## Slide 6: SAM Without YOLO (Second Attempt)
*   We tried using Meta's **Segment Anything Model (SAM)** directly on raw frames.
*   **Result:** SAM did "a little better" than Classic CV — it could sometimes capture a rough fish outline.
*   **Problem:** Without a YOLO bounding box to guide SAM, it didn't know *where* the fish was. It accidentally segmented tank equipment, bubbles, and reflections.
*   **Conclusion:** Not up to our expectations.

## Slide 7: Regression Models We Trained
*   Despite the masking issues, we trained **4 regression models** on the measurements from the frames that worked:
    1. **Linear Regression** — Simple baseline
    2. **Ridge Regression** — L2 regularized linear model (alpha=1.0)
    3. **Random Forest** — 200 trees, max_depth=8
    4. **Gradient Boosting** — 200 trees, max_depth=4, learning_rate=0.05
*   **Validation:** Leave-One-Fish-Out Cross Validation (train on 14 fish, predict the 15th)

## Slide 9: Why We Moved to YOLO
*   **The Core Problem:** Classic CV has no "intelligence" — it relies on background subtraction, which fails in murky water.
*   **What We Needed:** An AI model that can *learn* what a fish looks like and confidently locate it regardless of water conditions.
*   **Solution:** Train a YOLO object detection model on manually annotated frames.
*   **This leads to → The YOLO + Full SAM Pipeline (Next Presentation)**
